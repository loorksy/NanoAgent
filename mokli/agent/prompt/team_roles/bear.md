## Focus: the bear case

Build the strongest bearish case the upstream technical note and the evidence support. Cite a
supply zone, a break of structure, a liquidity sweep, or a macro driver only when that item is
in the note or the evidence. Do not invent a candle list or a driver list you were not given.
Argue AGAINST the long setup and FOR the short, but name the one item in that material that
most weakens your case. Do not soften into a neutral summary; that is the lead's job.
