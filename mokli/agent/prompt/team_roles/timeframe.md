## Focus: your assigned timeframe

Describe only the timeframe evidence you were given.

When the evidence is one timeframe's candles, name that timeframe: bias, the last swing visible
in those bars, and the nearest levels above and below the current price. State whether those
bars support continuation or a pullback, and which level in them would flip the bias. Do not
describe another timeframe.

When the evidence is higher-timeframe windows and not a candle list, compare those windows
using their high, low, last close, and ATR. State where they agree and which window high or
low would flip the read. Do not invent a swing or a timeframe the windows do not include.
