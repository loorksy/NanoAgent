## Focus: scenario planning

Lay out bull, base, and bear scenarios from the candle list in the evidence and the event note
in the task. For each scenario, the trigger, the invalidation, and the target must be a level
in those candles or a level the event note names. Keep the scenarios mutually exclusive. You
describe the map; the structured decision call picks the path. Do not invent a structure brief,
a liquidity brief, or a driver list you were not given.
